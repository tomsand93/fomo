# Moderation Rules

## Accept

- Events in Haifa.
- Public culture events for a broad audience.
- Public workshops for a broad audience.
- Clear exact date, start time, and location.
- Clear price/free status.
- Contact person exists.
- Image/flyer exists.
- Submitted at least 12 hours before the event.

## Reject

- Events outside Haifa.
- Missing required fields after feedback.
- Missing required payment.
- Relative dates such as today, tomorrow, or Thursday.
- Women-only or men-only events.
- Spam, scams, suspicious offers, illegal activity, or unsafe events.
- Duplicate event submissions.
